About ConnectTogether

Communication is not just about speaking or hearing; it is about expressing thoughts, understanding others and feeling connected.

ConnectTogether is an inclusive, AI-powered communication platform developed to bridge communication gaps faced by blind, deaf and mute individuals. Our platform brings together intelligent technologies to make everyday communication easier, more accessible and more independent.

We believe that technology should not create boundaries based on physical abilities. Instead, it should open new possibilities for everyone.

Our Philosophy

"Different Abilities, Same Right to Connect."

Every individual deserves the freedom to express, understand and participate in the world. ConnectTogether aims to make that possible through accessible digital solutions.

Every Ability Has a Way to Connect

ConnectTogether brings together intelligent communication tools that transform speech, text, images and sounds into accessible information. Choose your category, explore its features and experience a new way of connecting with the world.

👁️ 1. Features for Blind Individuals

"Experience the world beyond what your eyes can see."

1. Text to Speech

Turn written words into a listening experience. Simply enter or paste text and let the system read the information aloud, making digital content easier to access.

2. Image to Speech

Discover what an image communicates without needing to see it. The system analyses the uploaded image, generates a meaningful description and converts it into speech.

3. Sound Recognition

Stay informed about the sounds around you. The system identifies supported environmental sounds and provides meaningful audio or text alerts.

4. AI Object Detection

Explore your surroundings through intelligent vision technology. The camera identifies objects in front of you and announces their names through voice output.

5. Speech to Text

Convert spoken conversations into readable text. This feature helps users access verbal information through displayed words.

🦻 2. Features for Deaf Individuals

"When sound becomes text, communication becomes easier."

1. Image to Text

Bring written information from the physical world onto your screen. Extract words from photographs, documents and signboards using text recognition technology.

2. Speech to Text

Never miss the words spoken around you. Convert live conversations into readable text to make everyday communication more accessible.

3. AI Sound Recognition

Understand important sounds without depending on hearing. The system recognises supported environmental sounds and displays visual notifications to communicate what is happening.

4. Text to Image

Transform imagination into visuals. Enter a written description and use AI-powered image generation to create a corresponding picture.

🗣️ 3. Features for Mute Individuals

"Your thoughts deserve a voice, and your expressions deserve to be understood."

1. Text to Speech

Give your written thoughts a voice. Type your message, and the system converts it into spoken words so you can communicate with people around you.

2. Speech to Text

Make spoken conversations visible. Convert detected speech into written messages, helping users understand and participate in communication.

3. Image to Text

Turn visual words into digital information. Upload an image and extract its written content into editable, readable text.

4. Image to Speech

Let images communicate through voice. The system analyses an image, generates a description and reads the information aloud.

5. Text to Image

Express your imagination without speaking. Convert written descriptions into meaningful visual representations to communicate ideas creatively.

6. Live Camera

Capture the world as it happens. Use your device camera to capture live visual information and process it through supported image recognition features.
